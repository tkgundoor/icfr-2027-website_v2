Themes

Original research is invited in forensic chemistry, biology, physics, AI and nanotechnology.

Streams of interest

Conference themes

Original research is invited under the following forensic science disciplines. Related topics that advance evidence-based science are also welcome.

THEME A

Forensic Chemistry and Toxicology

  • Pharmaceutical Chemistry
  • Forensic Toxicology
  • Explosive Chemistry
  • Food and Agriculture
  • Fire and Arson Chemistry
  • NDPS and Novel Synthetic Drugs
  • Novel sensors for point-of-care detection
THEME B

Forensic Biology and Biotechnology

  • DNA fingerprinting
  • Forensic Serology
  • Forensic Anthropology
  • Forensic Odontology
  • Forensic Medicine
  • Omics in forensic science
THEME C

Forensic Physics and Ballistics

  • Forensic ballistics
  • Computer Forensics
  • Audio-video forensics
  • Forensic engineering
  • Fingerprint and Question Documents
THEME D

AI in Forensic Sciences

Integration of AI in allied domains of Forensic Science.

THEME E

Nanotechnology in Forensic Sciences

Application of Nanotechnology in allied domains of Forensic Science.
