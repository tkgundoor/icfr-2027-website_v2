Canine Forensics

About the workshop

A practical, multidisciplinary workshop for students, researchers, academicians and other key stakeholders from the region. Participants gain hands-on experience in crime scene investigation through canine forensics: detection and sample collection, preservation, documentation, report preparation and case filing.

Lectures are combined with field demonstrations. About 200 participants are expected.
