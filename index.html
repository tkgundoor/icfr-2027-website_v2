About the conference

The 1st International Conference on Forensic Research (ICFR-2027) is an international forum for advancing research, innovation, and interdisciplinary collaboration in forensic science and allied fields. It will unite researchers, forensic practitioners, law enforcement professionals, legal experts, policymakers, and industry representatives to address emerging challenges in forensic science to strengthen the criminal justice system.

The program includes keynote lectures, panel discussions, oral and poster presentations, and interactions with industry experts. Topics include forensic chemistry, toxicology, questioned documents, crime scene management and reconstruction, forensic biotechnology, forensic ballistics, laboratory innovations, and AI in allied domains of forensic science.

Participants will gain insight into modern analytical techniques, multidisciplinary case analyses, and evolving international standards while engaging in dialogue on evidence reliability, forensic interpretation, and the integration of advanced technologies into investigations.

Objectives

  • Facilitate the global platform to showcase pivotal forensic research, emerging technologies and novel investigative methodologies
  • Develop interdisciplinary partnerships uniting various stakeholders of forensic science.
  • Promote the integration of advanced forensic tools and evidence-based practices across modern investigative workflows.
  • Endorse rigorous validation and standardization to ensure the reliability, reproducibility, and judicial admissibility of forensic evidence.
